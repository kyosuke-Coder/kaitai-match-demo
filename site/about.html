About

Guides from inside the city, not above it.

Tokyo Field Notes is written by a Tokyo resident based in Shinjuku — a ten-minute walk from the world’s busiest station. Every route on this site is walked before it’s published, prices are checked in person, and guides carry the date they were last verified.

Why it matters: most Japan travel content is written from abroad and recycled year after year. Opening hours change, ticket rules change, whole markets move. Living here means we catch it — and it means the “local tips” are things residents actually do, not legends passed between blogs.

What we make:

  • Field-note guides — Tokyo first, plus the day trips locals take (Hakone, Kamakura, Nikko, Kawagoe).
  • Printable planners — trip planning tools built from the same walk-tested information.

Corrections and questions are welcome — if a price or hour has changed, tell us and we’ll fix it with credit.
